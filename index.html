06Good to know

Questions, answered.

What do you work on?

Utes, trucks, camper vans, pop-top campers and caravans, plus canvas and covers from around the home.

Do you do small repairs?

Yes. Zips, patches, fly screens and windows are all welcome.

Can you make something from my own idea?

Yes. Custom design is what we do. Bring a sketch, a photo or the item.

How do I get a price?

Send a photo and a few details through the form. Bigger builds are quoted after a look at the vehicle.

Is this the same business as True North Custom Canvas?

Yes. True North Custom Canvas is now Custom Canvas Australia.
